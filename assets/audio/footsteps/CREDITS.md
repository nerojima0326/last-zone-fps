# Footstep and material-contact samples (v79)

Kenney, **Impact Sounds**, CC0 1.0 Universal.
Original: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Mirror: https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot/tree/999dd1684873f8b020a3aa5b26e713da21688924

Only the 25 audio files listed in `sources.json` and the CC0 license are copied.
No plugin code is imported or executed. Original OGG files are unchanged; hashes
are recorded. Runtime playback normalizes peaks and uses small pitch variation.
Grass, concrete and wood use their named samples. Loose dirt/sand/gravel use
filtered snow/grass/concrete samples as documented in `js/footsteps.js`.
Metal adds a short light metal impact over the concrete sole contact.
Water keeps the game's synthesized splash fallback. These adaptations are not
claimed to be separate recordings of every ground type.
